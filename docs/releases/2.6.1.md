# Elsepin 2.6.1

Build 89 · 16 November 2026

## The big changes

- **Plan every leg.** Mix walking, running, cycling, e-bike and driving, each with its own speed and stops. Usual speeds apply to new routes; saved routes keep your choices.
- **Reuse the whole itinerary.** Recalculate in another mode without entering the stops again, or keep the original path. Queue a next route with an optional wait and automatic start.
- **Save and favorite separately.** Save to Library or Save & Favorite. The map star and Library stay in sync; unfavoriting keeps the saved item.
- **Make the map yours.** Choose pin symbols, emoji, colors, sizes and zoom visibility. Pins update while you zoom; area outlines and the map scale help you get your bearings.
- **More room to edit.** Larger maps, optional full screen, search in another city, and Undo/Redo for area corners.
- **Bring your settings with you.** Import a backup file or paste its text, then review what changes. Dev Mode lets you explore a separate demo library.

## Fixes that matter

Corrected saved-pin crashes, pairing cancellation/retry, lingering search bars, destination/Add stop controls and map-panel spacing. Pause and Stop stay reachable. Bike and e-bike routes try cycling directions, with a labeled walking-path fallback where available.

## Install

Export a backup, then sign and install **Elsepin-2.6.1.ipa** below. Wi-Fi, LocalDevVPN and a valid pairing connection are required; another device's Wi-Fi hotspot can also work.
