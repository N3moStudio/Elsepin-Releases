Which version is this?

This guide describes the 2.6.1 development app. The public 2.6.0 release may have different screens. Check its release notes and requirements before installing. Public release checked 2026-11-15.

1. Install on your iPhone

  1. If you already use Elsepin, export a library backup first. A different signing identity or deleting the app can affect access to existing data.
  2. Get the IPA from the official public release. Use your preferred signing tool or service and follow its installation instructions.
  3. Keep the companion extension if you want widgets or Live Activities. Those features also depend on the capabilities your signing setup provides.

There is no one signing setup this guide can guarantee will work. Follow the release’s requirements and the prompts on your device.

2. Pair and check the connection

  1. Join Wi-Fi for pairing. Install and connect LocalDevVPN, the local developer tunnel Elsepin uses.
  2. Open Elsepin and follow its setup checklist. Allow the requested local network access. The standard tunnel address is 10.7.0.1.
  3. Choose Pair on this iPhone when offered, and follow the in-app instructions for Developer Mode and the pairing code. Enter your device passcode only in the iPhone’s own system prompt.
  4. Complete Check connection. Keep LocalDevVPN connected when you start, move, or clear a session.
Using a pairing file instead?

Create an RPPairing file with idevice_pair on your computer, then choose the app’s import or paste pairing option. A SideStore pairing file uses a different format. Keep pairing files private; don’t include them in screenshots or shared backups.

3. Make your first move

Search for a nearby place or drop a pin. Inspect the selected location, then tap Teleport. Check your location in another app after Elsepin reports the update.

For a journey, open the route planner, choose a start and destination, and add any stops. Review the path, movement modes, and speeds. Tap Show route, then Start route on the map. Preparing or saving a route does not start movement.

Bike and e-bike path availability varies. Read any fallback notice when walking directions are used; a walking path is not a guarantee of cycling access.

Stop, clear, or reconnect

Pause stops progress along a route. Ending a route while holding its location keeps that simulated position. To return to normal location updates, use Stop spoofing and wait for confirmation.

If Elsepin can’t clear the location, connect to Wi-Fi, reconnect LocalDevVPN, and try again. Connecting to another device’s Wi-Fi hotspot can also work. Closing or force-quitting Elsepin doesn’t confirm that it cleared. Check the reported location in another app too.

Connection confirmed is not “every app updated.”

Elsepin can report its own connection and update attempts. Other apps may cache an older position or update on their own schedule.

Make it feel like yours

Open Settings → Appearance to choose from eight themes. Bloom is the default. Each theme determines its light or dark palette; Glacier follows your device. Choose solid or glass surfaces. Font design and the app’s base text size are consistent across themes, with accessibility handled by the app’s native layout.

App icons are a separate choice. The appearance studio uses the approved artwork and the app’s palette colors, but its map and screens are illustrations.

Widgets and signing

Saved-location widgets need shared App Group access, and the extension must remain in your signed installation. A signing service can change or remove those permissions. An “unavailable” message in the app is more useful than what the website preview shows. Widget actions prepare a place; they still require confirmation in Elsepin before movement.

Updates and backups

Use Settings → Software updates to check official releases. Downloading an update remains your choice. Export a library backup before changing your installation, then review the selected changes when importing it.

Optional provider checks

These require a separately configured helper and your own provider account. A check shows the provider’s reported location and how old the observation is. It can be stale; a match is not proof that every app has refreshed.

Find My Devices and Find My People are different services. People support remains experimental and is not a promised working feature. Keep helper tokens and account credentials out of screenshots, public repositories, and shared files.
