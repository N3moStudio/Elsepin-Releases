Just a preview.

This website does not request your location, access your phone, collect account credentials, or connect to a location provider. Its maps and session controls are illustrations.

What the page stores

Theme, icon, and demo selections exist only in page memory. They reset when you reload. There are no analytics scripts, tracking pixels, advertising cookies, external font requests, or account forms in the site code.

Hosting and external links

The hosting provider may process ordinary request information, such as IP addresses, browser details, timestamps, and requested paths. External sites linked in the setup guide have their own privacy practices.

The iPhone app is separate

The app can store places, routes, settings, and pairing information on your device. Optional helpers connect to external providers only after separate setup. This page’s privacy note describes the website, not those provider services.
