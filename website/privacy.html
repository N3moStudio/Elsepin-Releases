Choices stay on this page

Theme, icon, screen, and simulated-session selections exist only in the page’s memory. Reloading resets them. The website code does not write cookies or browser storage, ask for account credentials, or send preview choices to a server.

There are no analytics scripts, tracking pixels, advertising integrations, account forms, or geolocation requests in the site. Fonts and artwork load from the same website, not a third-party font service.

Hosting and external links

The hosting provider may process ordinary request information, including IP addresses, browser details, timestamps, and requested paths. This site’s code does not control the host’s log retention.

Links to GitHub, the App Store, and other external resources take you to services with their own privacy practices. Those services are not embedded in the appearance preview.

The app is separate

The iPhone app can store your pins, routes, areas, settings, and pairing information. Optional provider helpers need separate configuration. This note describes the website; it is not a privacy policy for Apple, Life360, a signing service, or a helper you host.

Keep pairing files, account credentials, and helper tokens private. See the setup guide for the connection and signing requirements.

Artwork and credits

Elsepin’s approved icon collection is shown without redrawing or recoloring it. The website’s maps are illustrative diagrams, not Apple Maps or live location data.
