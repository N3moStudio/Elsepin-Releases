A few things
worth knowing.

The useful things to know before you begin.

Does this website change my location?

No. The map and session buttons are an illustration. The site does not request your location, connect to your iPhone, or start a real session.

What do I need to use the app?

An iPhone with a compatible signed installation, LocalDevVPN, and a valid pairing. Signing permissions and iOS behavior matter; a downloaded IPA alone is not a complete setup. Follow the setup guide and the release’s requirements.

Does “update sent” mean every app has updated?

No. Session status describes Elsepin’s own connection and updates. Another app may still show an older location. Optional provider checks show a reported location and its age; they do not guarantee a fresh update. Find My Devices and Find My People are different, and People support is not a promised working feature.

How do I return to my real location?

Use Stop spoofing and wait for confirmation. If Elsepin can’t clear the location, connect to Wi-Fi, reconnect LocalDevVPN, and try again. Connecting to another device’s Wi-Fi hotspot can also work. Closing or force-quitting Elsepin doesn’t confirm that it cleared. Check the location in another app too.

Can I keep my pins, routes, and favorites?

The app has a saved library and backup import/export. Before changing your installation or signing identity, export a backup. Imports let you review individual changes; keep pairing files private and separate.

Why might widgets or icons behave differently?

Your signing setup must retain the extension and supported capabilities. Saved-location widgets need shared App Group access. Re-signing can affect those permissions and alternate icons; the website preview cannot verify your installation.

What does the website store?

Preview choices stay in page memory and reset on reload. The site code has no analytics, account forms, or location requests. Hosting providers may retain normal request logs. Read the website privacy note.
